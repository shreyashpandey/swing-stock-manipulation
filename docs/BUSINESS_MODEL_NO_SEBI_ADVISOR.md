Last updated: 2026-08-03
Status: active product direction

## Purpose

This document defines the business model SwingDesk can pursue **right now** without:

- a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (IA)
- a SEBI-registered Research Analyst (RA)
- a registered advisory partner

The goal is to keep the product commercially viable while staying on the **analytics software** side of the line rather than the **investment advice** side.

This is a product and operating document, not legal advice. Before public launch, all website copy, app labels, onboarding, pricing pages, and growth content should be reviewed by Indian counsel familiar with SEBI IA/RA rules.

## The Core Rule

If we do not have a SEBI-registered advisor/research analyst attached to the product, then SwingDesk must be sold as:

- software
- analytics
- research workflow
- education
- information presentation

SwingDesk must **not** be sold as:

- investment advice
- stock recommendation service
- buy/sell call product
- portfolio advisory
- capital allocation service
- managed or quasi-managed trading assistance

## Market Position

The closest safe market analogs are analytics products such as:

- Tickertape
- Screener
- Trendlyne
- StockEdge
- Chartink

The closest unsafe analogs for our current regulatory posture are advisory-style products that issue recommendations or personalized action guidance.

Therefore the correct market position is:

**SwingDesk is a market analytics and research workbench for Indian active investors and swing traders.**

Recommended one-line positioning:

> SwingDesk helps users discover, evaluate, and track opportunities using rule-based scanners, rankings, historical analysis, and portfolio diagnostics. Users make their own decisions.

## What We Can Sell Now

The monetizable product today is a **subscription analytics platform**.

### Sellable product categories

- market scanners
- screeners and rankings
- unusual-activity / manipulation analytics
- event and catalyst monitoring
- portfolio diagnostics
- paper trading and journaling
- backtests and parameter labs
- user-defined alerts
- exports and saved workspaces
- educational content and workflow templates

### Non-sellable product categories without advisory registration

- stock recommendations
- public "top picks" framed as advice
- personalized buy/sell/hold opinions
- target/stop recommendations framed as action instructions
- user-specific allocation into named securities
- "we recommend this trade today" content
- discretionary or semi-discretionary portfolio actions

## Product Principles

Every user-facing feature should pass these tests:

### 1. User-controlled, not product-directed

Allowed:

- user selects filters and sees matches
- user configures watchlists and alerts
- user chooses the stock, amount, and horizon

Avoid:

- app picks the stock and tells the user to act
- app auto-ranks "best stocks to buy now" with prescriptive language

### 2. Descriptive, not prescriptive

Allowed:

- "Volume is 5.2x the 30-day average"
- "This stock matches your breakout screen"
- "Historical probability of touching +8% within 20 sessions is 37%"

Avoid:

- "Buy this stock"
- "Sell this now"
- "This is our recommended target"

### 3. Generic analytics, not personalized advice

Allowed:

- same scanner results visible to all users
- user-operated calculators
- generic rankings and diagnostics

Avoid:

- "Given your holdings, you should sell X and buy Y"
- "Invest ₹50,000 into this stock"
- "Your best move today is..."

## Suggested Pricing For India

Start with low-friction annual pricing and a freemium funnel.

### Free

- limited watchlist
- limited scans
- limited unusual-activity preview
- delayed or capped alerts
- no exports

### Pro

Target price:

- `₹2,999/year`
- optional `₹299/month`

Includes:

- full screener access
- full unusual-activity scanner
- saved screens
- portfolio analytics
- backtests
- alerts
- exports

### Trader / Edge

Target price:

- `₹7,999–₹9,999/year`

Includes:

- intraday workflows
- deeper alerting
- scanner history
- advanced lab features
- more workspace and export capability

### Launch offer

- `₹1,999/year` for early adopters

This keeps us competitive with Indian analytics platforms while giving us room to move up once the wedge is proven.

## The Product Wedge

SwingDesk should not market itself as a generic "stock market app."

The sharpest launch wedge is:

**India's unusual-activity and swing research workbench**

This wedge is stronger because it:

- differentiates from broad retail finance tools
- gives us a clear paid feature anchor
- is compatible with a tool-only regulatory posture
- maps well to current app capabilities

## Disclaimer Stack

Disclaimers are necessary but not sufficient. Product behavior must also remain tool-like.

### 1. Blocking onboarding disclaimer

Use this on first launch or signup:

> SwingDesk is an analytics and research software platform for informational and educational purposes only. We are not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser, Research Analyst, Portfolio Manager, or stock broker. Nothing on this platform constitutes investment advice, a recommendation, a research report, a buy/sell/hold call, or a solicitation to trade in any security. All data, rankings, alerts, screeners, probabilities, expected ranges, and AI-generated summaries are general in nature and are not personalized to your financial situation, objectives, or risk tolerance. You are solely responsible for your investment and trading decisions. Markets involve risk, including possible loss of capital. By continuing, you acknowledge that you understand and accept this.

### 2. Persistent footer disclaimer

Use on every screen:

> SwingDesk provides analytical tools and information for educational purposes only. It is not investment advice. We are not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser or Research Analyst. All decisions are made by the user at their own risk. Some outputs are AI-assisted.

### 3. Analytics page banner

Use on scanners, screeners, rankings, ML, expected range, and similar pages:

> Rule-based analytics only. This output is descriptive and informational, not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security.

### 4. Backtest and performance disclaimer

Use anywhere returns, hit rates, expectancy, or performance are shown:

> Backtests, simulations, and historical performance are hypothetical and based on past data. They do not guarantee future results.

### 5. Legal page disclosure points

The website/app legal hub should clearly state:

- not a SEBI-registered IA
- not a SEBI-registered RA
- not a broker
- not a PMS
- not tax, legal, or accounting advice
- AI outputs may be incomplete or incorrect
- third-party data may be delayed or incomplete
- users must verify and decide independently

## Phase Plan

### Phase 1: Analytics software only

- subscriptions
- scanners
- portfolio tooling
- research lab
- no advisory

### Phase 2: Optional later expansion

Only if we choose to:

- obtain our own SEBI registration, or
- partner with a registered RA/IA, or
- create a separate advisory layer with compliant controls

## Decision Summary

For now, SwingDesk should operate as:

**A subscription analytics platform for Indian active investors and swing traders, centered on scanners, rankings, portfolio diagnostics, and backtesting, without recommendations or personalized advice.**
